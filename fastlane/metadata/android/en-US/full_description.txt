FOSStenbuch is a free and open source trip logbook for Android – for individuals, freelancers and businesses who want to document their trips easily and privately. Your trips are stored only on your device. The app is available in German.

<b>Features</b>

* Start and end trips – start and destination, odometer readings, category (business/private), business partner, route and vehicle. New trips are recorded while you drive, not added afterwards; changes to completed trips are logged, and completed trips cannot be deleted, only cancelled
* GPS distance – measures the distance driven during the trip (as an estimate; the odometer reading counts)
* Automatic trip detection – when your phone connects to the car's Bluetooth, the trip is recorded; you review and accept it later. For this the app uses location in the background, even when it is closed (only if you set up detection)
* Multiple vehicles, custom categories, saved locations and templates for recurring trips
* Edit lock ("Bearbeitungssperre") per vehicle, cannot be undone – completed trips can only be cancelled; a hash chain checks backups on restore
* A seal for every trip that is ended, accepted, edited or cancelled – optionally signed with your own key and confirmed by a timestamp from an independent service; signed PDFs and backups can be checked in the browser on our verification page
* Export as CSV, PDF or detailed PDF per vehicle with odometer readings, change log and seals; backup and restore
* Statistics and a calculator for the German commuting allowance (German tax law, for guidance only)
* Daily reminder to log your trips
* Austrian and German licence plates
* Light and dark theme

Whether the tax office accepts a logbook depends on how you keep it. The app gives no guarantee and is no substitute for tax advice. Tips on correct use (in German): https://github.com/GARSKE-SYSTEMS/FOSStenbuch#f%C3%BCr-das-finanzamt

<b>Privacy</b>

* No account, no ads, no analytics or tracking services
* Online features: address search and place names via OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) – your search term or the start and end coordinates of automatically detected trips are sent to the OpenStreetMap Foundation. Optional timestamps (off by default) – only a SHA-256 fingerprint per seal is sent to the selected timestamp service.
* Your signing key stays encrypted on the device; the app never sends it anywhere
* Free software under the MIT license: https://github.com/GARSKE-SYSTEMS/FOSStenbuch
